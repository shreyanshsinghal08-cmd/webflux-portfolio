My dearest Bhabhi

Some people enter a family by marriage. You entered ours and made it feel complete — as if the missing warmth had simply been waiting for your name.

You have a rare gift: of making everyone around you feel seen, cared for, and quietly celebrated. Your kindness never announces itself. It lives in the way you remember the little things, the way you hold this family together with grace, and the way your smile can turn an ordinary evening into something golden.

On this thirteenth of September, I hope the universe returns even a fraction of the gentleness you give so freely. May this year wrap you in health that glows, dreams that bloom, and days that feel as beautiful as you make ours.
