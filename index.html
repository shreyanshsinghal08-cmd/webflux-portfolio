Answers & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our web design process, pricing, turnaround times, and delivery guarantees.

How much does a website design cost?

We provide transparent pricing in INR: Basic Plan starts at ₹10,000 (2 pages, 3-day turnaround), Standard Plan is ₹30,000 (5 pages, CMS, free demo before payment), and Premium Plan is ₹50,000 for advanced institutions. Luxury creator media kits start at 99 AED to 799 AED.

Do I get a free demo before making final payment?

Yes, absolutely. On Standard and Premium packages, we build a fully functional live demo website first. You get to review the layout, mobile responsiveness, and design before releasing payment.

What is the typical project delivery timeline?

Most school, coaching, and business websites are delivered within 7 to 14 days. Basic 2-page websites are delivered in just 3 days with zero compromise on quality.

Are the websites mobile-friendly and optimized for SEO?

Every website is built with a mobile-first architecture, guaranteeing smooth 60fps scrolling and zero horizontal overflow. We implement structured JSON-LD schema, metadata, and Core Web Vitals optimization to rank on Google and AI search engines.

Do you work with international clients outside India?

Yes! We collaborate with global clients, including creators and brands in Dubai and the UAE (such as the Dubai Viibe luxury media kit), providing multi-currency billing and seamless WhatsApp communication.
